03 · LIGHT MEASUREMENT

LDR (Photoresistor) Sensor

An LDR changes resistance as the light falling on it changes. A voltage-divider circuit converts that resistance change into a voltage an Arduino analog input can measure.

STEP 4 · DEBUG

Troubleshoot unreliable sensor readings

Work through these checks before changing several parts of a circuit at once.

1. Confirm power and ground

Check the module's rated supply voltage, verify polarity, and ensure sensor and controller grounds are connected when required.

2. Test one signal at a time

Use a small sketch or simple meter measurement to confirm the module changes output as the target or light level changes.

3. Account for the environment

Target material, angle, sunlight, nearby objects and electrical noise can all affect readings. Test under the conditions where the project will run.
