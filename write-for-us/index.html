Contributor guidelines

Make every article genuinely useful.

Our readers come to learn, solve a problem or complete a project. Write for that purpose first.

  • Submit original work written for Vayuyaan. Do not republish an existing article or lightly rewrite another publisher’s content.
  • Explain the steps in a clear order and define technical terms when needed.
  • For project tutorials, include a parts list, wiring or circuit details, working code where relevant, setup steps, testing instructions and expected results.
  • Use accurate technical information and cite trustworthy sources for claims, diagrams or data.
  • Use your own images or assets you have permission to publish.
  • Keep links relevant and useful to readers. Promotional or affiliate links must be disclosed and approved.
  • Use a clear title, descriptive H2/H3 headings, concise paragraphs, useful examples and a logical step-by-step structure. Answer the reader’s main question early.
  • Include a short author bio and disclose any commercial relationship that could affect the article.

Editorial standards

We may edit accepted submissions for clarity, structure, accuracy and house style. We may decline content that is duplicated, misleading, thin, overly promotional or unrelated to our readers. Sending a pitch does not guarantee publication.

Article length and quality

There is no fixed word count. Aim for the length needed to answer the topic completely without repetition. Add original analysis, practical detail, examples or testing notes instead of padding the article to reach a target.

Links & anchor text

Use descriptive, relevant backlink anchor text.

Links must help readers understand what they will find on the destination page. We review every external link in context; a requested anchor text is not automatically approved.

Examples of useful anchor text

  • “Arduino Uno R3 development board” — use only when the destination genuinely describes or sells that board.
  • “HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor guide” — suitable for a relevant tutorial explaining the sensor’s wiring or operation.
  • “ESP32 development board setup” — appropriate when the linked page provides setup instructions.
  • “electronics components for DIY projects” — suitable for a relevant category page when the surrounding sentence supports it.

Anchor text we avoid

Avoid vague phrases such as “click here,” “this website” or “read more” when a descriptive phrase would help. Also avoid forcing exact-match keywords into unrelated sentences, repeating the same commercial anchor unnaturally, or linking to a page that does not match the text.

Editorial link policy

Keep links relevant, limited to what genuinely helps the reader, and placed naturally in the article. We do not guarantee a particular anchor text, link placement, do-follow attribute or search ranking. Paid or sponsored links must be disclosed and handled with appropriate link attributes under applicable search-engine guidance.

How it works

From pitch to publication.

Start with a clear idea. We will review the topic before you prepare a full draft.

1. Send a pitch

Share your working title, target reader, short outline, proposed sources and why the topic will help readers.

2. Wait for review

We assess topic fit, originality, technical value, source quality and link relevance. Please do not send a full draft unless requested.

3. Submit your draft

If the pitch is approved, submit the draft with supporting sources, original images or diagrams, code where relevant, and a short author bio.

Ready to contribute?

Send us your article idea.

Include your proposed title, intended audience, a short outline, a brief author bio and one or two examples of your work. If you are requesting an external link, explain how the destination directly supports the article.

Every pitch and external link is subject to editorial review. Submission does not guarantee publication or a backlink.
