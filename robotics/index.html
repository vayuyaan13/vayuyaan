BUILD CHECKLIST

Before powering the robot

A short hardware checklist can save hours of debugging.

Power

Check polarity, battery condition and whether the supply can provide the motors' startup current. Do not power motors directly from microcontroller I/O pins.

Wiring

Check the pin map, secure motor terminals and common ground where required. Disconnect power before changing connections.

Software

Test one motor, one sensor and one behavior at a time. Log sensor readings and use low motor speed during initial tests.
