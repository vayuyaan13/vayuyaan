TROUBLESHOOTING

Common first-upload issues

Symptoms vary by board and USB interface, so check the exact board documentation too.

Upload fails to connect

Confirm the selected board and serial port. Some boards require you to hold a BOOT button during connection; follow the instructions for your specific model.

Port is missing

Try a known data-capable USB cable, reconnect the board, and install the appropriate USB-to-serial driver where required.

Peripheral behaves incorrectly

Verify pin availability, logic voltage, current needs and the board's pin restrictions. Not every pin supports every function on every board.
